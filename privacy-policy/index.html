Privacy policy

Updated October 28, 2026

Your app records

SnackSmart stores the records you create on your device. You do not need to create a SnackSmart account. Records and exported copies may also be included in backups or destinations you manage. Deleting the app does not remove copies you previously exported or retained in backups.

Purchases

Apple handles App Store payments and purchase transactions. The app uses purchase status to provide paid features. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

Advertising

The free app integrates Google AdMob. The advertising service may process device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, usage information and diagnostics to deliver and measure ads, and may use data for tracking as described in the App Store privacy disclosure and your consent or device settings. Premium provides an ad-free experience. See Google’s privacy policy and Google’s advertising information. Manage available tracking permissions in iOS Settings.

Optional intelligent features

Supported Apple Intelligence text interpretation uses an available on-device model. You review the result before applying or saving it. Model availability depends on your device and Apple settings; ordinary manual controls remain available.

Nutrition, photos and Health

Nutrition lookup can send the food search text to USDA FoodData Central when a local match is unavailable. Network requests also disclose ordinary connection information such as your IP address to that service. See USDA’s privacy policy. Photos and voice input use the permissions you grant. Apple Health access is optional and limited to the nutrition and water categories you authorize. You can change permissions in iOS Settings or Health. Nutrition estimates are not medical advice.

Exports and support

When you export, copy or share records, the destination you choose handles those copies. If you email support, your email address and the information you provide are used to respond. Avoid sending private records that are not needed to explain an issue.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information when serving these pages. See GitHub’s privacy statement. The screenshot gallery does not send your interactions to an analytics service.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information through support.
