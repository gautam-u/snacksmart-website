How can we help?

Contact gautam.ux@gmail.com with the app version, device model, iOS version and a short description of what happened. Please leave out private records, payment details and anything you do not want to share.

Common questions

Are nutrition values exact?

No. Nutrition values can be estimates. Check the portion and packaging, and edit values when better information is available. Only snacks you record contribute to your totals.

Can I use it without Apple Intelligence?

Yes. Direct entry, recent-snack shortcuts and manual editing work without model inference. Supported note interpretation requires a compatible device and an available model.

Will choosing an alternative log it?

Choose an option first, then use Log this when you want to record it. Satisfaction feedback is optional.

Purchases and subscriptions

Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Manage or cancel an auto-renewing subscription in your Apple Account settings. The App Store handles purchases and refund requests.
